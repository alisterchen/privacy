Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

Coffee Notes does not collect, store, or share any personal information. All data you enter stays on your device.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any information from users of Coffee Notes. The app functions entirely offline and does not transmit any data to external servers.

Data Storage

All data you create within Coffee Notes — including your tasting notes, bean records, brew logs, and photos — is stored locally on your device only. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not backed up to any cloud service operated by us
  • Is not accessible to the developer or any third party
  • Is removed when you uninstall the app or clear app data

Photos and Camera

If you choose to add photos to your notes, Coffee Notes accesses your camera or photo library only to retrieve the selected image. Photos are stored locally within the app's private storage on your device and are not uploaded or shared.

Third-Party Services

Coffee Notes does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No data is shared with any third party for any purpose.

Children's Privacy

Coffee Notes does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children. Since no data is collected at all, no special handling for children is required.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the new version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
